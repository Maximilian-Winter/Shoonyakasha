# Stage

A light show over a turntable: one of the showroom's cars or starships on a stage deck in a dark hall full of haze and smoke, under a truss of moving heads, with a tribune facing the stage.

```
python stage.py
python stage.py --models corvette_c8 --programme circles --colours rainbow
python stage.py --screenshots shots/       stills of every programme, then exit
```

It needs a GPU with ray queries: the fog's light comes through rays to each light.

## What is on stage

- **The model:** the showroom's models (`python tools/fetch_assets.py showroom`, see the [showroom's README](../showroom/README.md)), on its turntable, with their clear coat. Without them it shows the bundled Fox. Models larger than a car scale the whole set with them.
- **Moving heads:** twelve spot lights with ray-traced shadows, eight hanging from the truss and four standing on the deck. Each head and its yoke turn to where the programme points it, easing there, and its lens glows in its colour.
- **Key lights:** two spot lights on the truss's front corners keep the model lit whatever the moving heads do. They light surfaces only, not the fog.
- **Smoke:** haze through the whole hall, ground fog over the floor, and four smoke machines, two on the deck and two on the floor in front of it, puffing plumes across the stage. The fog shows only where light reaches it, so the beams appear in the air and the truss and the model cut shadows into them.
- **Lasers:** four laser projectors, two on the deck behind the model aiming over the audience and two under the middle of the truss's front and back beams. Their beams are traced to what they hit, light the haze and smoke as thin lines, and leave dots where they land; a sheet of light draws a line across whatever it crosses.
- **The set:** the hall, deck, tribune, truss, moving heads and smoke machines are generated by `stage_set.py` on first start into `assets/stage/set/`; delete that directory to generate them again.

The pipeline is the showroom's plus the fog and the lasers; its [README](pipeline/README.md) describes their passes and settings.

## The show

- **Programmes** move the heads: *Sweep* swings them across the deck, the truss's front and back rows crossing over the model; *Fan* opens and closes them sideways; *Circles* turns each round its own circle; *Columns* points them straight down and up; *Audience* swings them towards the tribune; *Focus* puts every head on the model.
- **Colours:** *White*, *Ice*, *Sunset*, *Rainbow* (a hue per head, drifting) and *Ultraviolet*. The deck's light strip and the turntable's rim take the first head's colour.
- **Laser shows:** *Fans* of beams from three projectors, tilting and rolling; *Waves*, fans whose beams rise and fall one after another; *Tunnel*, beams on turning cones, which from the audience look like stars; *Sky*, a sheet of light over the audience rippling in the haze, with two small fans; *Everything*; and *Off*.
- **Laser colours:** *Green*, *RGB* (a colour per projector), *Cyan/magenta*, *Rainbow* and *As the heads*.

The settings panel at the left sets the programme and colours, their speed, the heads' brightness and beam width, the key lights, the laser show, its colours and power, the haze, the smoke machines' output, the ground fog, how brightly the fog glows and how much it scatters forward, the camera and the exposure.

## Keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| LEFT / RIGHT | Previous / next model |
| SPACE | Next programme |
| K | Next colours |
| L | Next laser show |
| J | Next laser colours |
| C | Next camera: audience (from the tribune), stage (round the deck), orbit (drag with the left mouse button, scroll to zoom), free (WASD/Q/E, right mouse) |
| T | Turntable on or off |
| G | Fog on or off |
| O | Frame rate and the fog passes' GPU time in the status line |
| F2 | Settings panel on or off |
| P | Screenshot into `stage_captures/` |
| F9 | Start or stop recording a video into `stage_captures/` |

## Options

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `--models` | every fetched model | Comma-separated showroom catalogue names, in order |
| `--programme` | `sweep` | Movement to open with |
| `--colours` | `ice` | Colours to open with |
| `--lasers` | `fans` | Laser show to open with |
| `--laser-colours` | `rgb` | Laser colours to open with |
| `--camera` | `audience` | Camera to open with |
| `--width`, `--height` | 1920, 1080 | Window size |
| `--texture-size` | 4096 | Largest texture side to load |
| `--screenshots DIR` | | Save stills of the first model, then exit: every programme from the audience and from the stage with the `--lasers` show, then every other laser show from the audience; each with its GPU time and the fog's and lasers' share |
| `--settle` | 150 | Frames each still runs, at 1/60 s each, before it is saved |
| `--fps` | 30 | Frame rate of recordings |
