# Stage

A light show over a turntable: one of the showroom's cars or starships on a stage deck in a dark hall full of haze and smoke, under a truss of moving heads and lasers, with a tribune facing the stage. It opens running a show on a beat clock, the camera cutting between views with the music.

```
python stage.py
python stage.py --show club --bpm 128
python stage.py --show none --programme circles --colours rainbow --lasers tunnel
python stage.py --screenshots shots/       stills of every programme and laser show, then exit
python stage.py --record show.mp4 --seconds 60
```

It needs a GPU with ray queries: the fog's light comes through rays to each light.

## What is on stage

- **The model:** the showroom's models (`python tools/fetch_assets.py showroom`, see the [showroom's README](../showroom/README.md)), on its turntable, with their clear coat. Without them it shows the bundled Fox. Models larger than a car scale the whole set with them.
- **Moving heads:** twelve spot lights with ray-traced shadows, eight hanging from the truss and four standing on the deck. Each head and its yoke turn to where the programme points it, easing there, and its lens glows in its colour.
- **Key lights:** two spot lights on the truss's front corners keep the model lit whatever the moving heads do. They light surfaces only, not the fog.
- **Smoke:** haze through the whole hall, ground fog over the floor, and four smoke machines, two on the deck and two on the floor in front of it, puffing plumes across the stage. The fog shows only where light reaches it, so the beams appear in the air and the truss and the model cut shadows into them.
- **Lasers:** four laser projectors, two on the deck behind the model aiming over the audience and two under the middle of the truss's front and back beams. Their beams are traced to what they hit, light the haze and smoke as thin lines, and leave dots where they land; a sheet of light draws a line across whatever it crosses.
- **The set:** the hall, deck, tribune, truss, moving heads, smoke machines and laser projectors are generated by `stage_set.py` on first start into `assets/stage/set/`; delete that directory to generate them again.

The pipeline is the showroom's plus the fog and the lasers; its [README](pipeline/README.md) describes their passes and settings.

## The show

Everything runs on a beat clock: the tempo slider sets it, or four taps or more of **B** (or the panel's tap button), each tap less than two seconds after the last; the beat then falls on the latest tap. The movements and colour drifts go at a speed set by the tempo times the movement speed slider.

- **Movement programmes:** *Sweep* swings the heads across the deck, the truss's front and back rows crossing over the model; *Fan* opens and closes them sideways; *Circles* turns each round its own circle; *Columns* points them straight down and up; *Audience* swings them towards the tribune; *Focus* puts every head on the model; *Manual* points them all where the pan and tilt sliders say.
- **Colours:** *White*, *Ice*, *Sunset*, *Rainbow* (a hue per head, drifting) and *Ultraviolet*. The deck's light strip and the turntable's rim take the first head's colour.
- **Effects on the beat:** the dimmer is *Steady*, *Pulse* (every head flashes on the beat and fades), *Chase* (one head after another, two a beat) or *Alternate* (every other head, swapping each beat); the strobe flashes the heads twice (*1/2*) or four times (*1/4*) a beat. The lasers can blank on the off-beat, and the smoke machines can burst on each bar, the burst travelling out along their plumes.
- **Laser shows:** *Fans* of beams from three projectors, tilting and rolling; *Waves*, fans whose beams rise and fall one after another; *Tunnel*, beams on turning cones, which from the audience look like stars; *Sky*, a sheet of light over the audience rippling in the haze, with two small fans; *Everything*; and *Off*.
- **Laser colours:** *Green*, *RGB* (a colour per projector), *Cyan/magenta*, *Rainbow* and *As the heads*.

**Shows** step through cues, each a few bars of a programme, colours, laser show, laser colours and effects, and start over after the last:

| Show | Cues |
|---|---|
| *Reveal* | Seven, 26 bars: dim ultraviolet columns with smoke bursts, every head on the model in white, then sweeps, circles, fans, strobes and the lasers building to everything |
| *Club* | Five of two bars each: chases, strobes and lasers on the beat |
| *Calm* | Three of eight bars each: slow sweeps, the sky sheet, the model in white |

A show starts on the next bar. Setting the movement, colours, effects or lasers by hand, on the panel or with the keys, stops it and keeps what it had set.

The settings panel at the left runs the shows and sets the tempo, the heads (programme, colours, dimmer, strobe, movement speed, brightness, beam width, manual pan and tilt), the key lights, the lasers (show, colours, blanking, power), the smoke (bursts, haze, the machines' output, ground fog, how brightly the fog glows and how much it scatters forward), the model, the camera and the exposure. The status line at the top shows the tempo, the beat in the bar, and the show's cue; the model's credit is at the bottom left.

## Recording

`--record FILE` runs the show for `--settle` frames, then records `--seconds` of it at `--fps` with a fixed step: every frame moves the show on by exactly one frame's time, so the video plays at the show's own speed however slowly it renders. The panel and the status line are hidden; the model's credit stays. Esc or Ctrl+C stops early and keeps what is recorded. **F9** records the window as it is, in real time.

## Cameras

| Camera | View |
|---|---|
| Director | Cuts to the next of middle seats, stage, front row, side, back row and stage every four bars |
| Middle seats, Front row, Back row | From the tribune, drifting slowly sideways |
| Side | From the side of the hall |
| Stage | Round the deck, low, near the model |
| Orbit | Drag with the left mouse button, scroll to zoom |
| Free | WASD/Q/E, right mouse to look |

## Keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| LEFT / RIGHT | Previous / next model |
| R | Run the show or stop it |
| N | Next show |
| B | Tap the tempo |
| SPACE | Next movement programme |
| K | Next colours |
| L | Next laser show |
| J | Next laser colours |
| C | Next camera |
| T | Turntable on or off |
| G | Fog on or off |
| O | Frame rate and the fog's and lasers' GPU time in the status line |

| P | Screenshot into `stage_captures/` |
| F9 | Start or stop recording a video into `stage_captures/` |

## Options

| Option | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `--models` | every fetched model | Comma-separated showroom catalogue names, in order |
| `--show` | `reveal` | Show to run from the start; `none` starts by hand |
| `--bpm` | 124 | Tempo of the beat clock |
| `--programme` | `sweep` | Movement to open with |
| `--colours` | `ice` | Colours to open with |
| `--lasers` | `fans` | Laser show to open with |
| `--laser-colours` | `rgb` | Laser colours to open with |
| `--camera` | `director` | Camera to open with |
| `--width`, `--height` | 1920, 1080 | Window size |
| `--texture-size` | 4096 | Largest texture side to load |
| `--screenshots DIR` | | Save stills of the first model, then exit: every programme from the middle seats and from the stage with the `--lasers` show, then every other laser show from the middle seats; each with its GPU time and the fog's and lasers' share. No show runs |
| `--record FILE` | | Record the show (`.mp4`, `.mkv`), then exit |
| `--seconds` | 40 | Length of the recording |
| `--settle` | 150 | Frames each still runs (at 1/60 s each) before it is saved, and a recording (at 1/`--fps` s each) before it starts |
| `--fps` | 30 | Frame rate of recordings |
